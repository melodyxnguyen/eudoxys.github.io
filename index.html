Welcome to Eudoxys℠

Eudoxys Sciences LLC is an independent consulting group that provides models and simulations to help the electric power industry plan for an increasingly uncertain and unpredictable future. We serve electric utilities, regulators, reliability organizations, and government agencies using physics-based and data-driven modeling techniques built on GridLAB-D.

Modeling

We develop high-fidelity static, quasi-steady, and dynamic load models for GridLAB-D, CYME, PSLF, and PSS/E — built from physics-based models, end-use surveys, and measured power data.

Learn More

Simulations

We prepare and validate GridLAB-D transmission and distribution simulations of all sizes, run on optimized AWS infrastructure and validated against AMI and SCADA data.

Learn More

Analysis

We conduct reliability and resilience analysis including IEEE 1366 metrics, electrification impact studies, conservation voltage reduction, and distribution-level optimal power flow.

Learn More

Forecasting

We develop short-term (1 hour to 1 week) and long-term (5–20 year) load forecasts for all major electric customer sectors from local to national scale.

Learn More

Controls

We develop and validate advanced load and distributed resource control strategies, with a focus on transactive energy solutions that use price signals to optimally dispatch distributed resources.

Learn More

Training

We provide two-hour training courses for utility engineers, academic faculty, and regulatory staff covering GridLAB-D modeling, simulation, Python APIs, and LF Energy tools.
