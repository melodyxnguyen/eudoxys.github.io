Making sense of our energy future

Eudoxys Sciences LLC is an independent consulting group providing models and simulations to help the electric power industry plan for an increasingly uncertain and unpredictable future.

Get a Quote Learn More

What We Do

We serve electric utilities, regulators, reliability organizations, and government agencies with physics-based and data-driven modeling.

Modeling

High-fidelity load models for GridLAB-D, CYME, PSLF, and PSS/E using physics-based models, end-use surveys, and measured data.

Simulations

GridLAB-D transmission and distribution simulations validated against AMI and SCADA data, run on optimized AWS infrastructure.

Analysis

System reliability, resilience, tariff design, electrification impacts, conservation voltage reduction, and optimal power flow.

Forecasting

Short-term and long-term load forecasts for all major electric customer sectors, from local to national scale.

Controls

Advanced load and distributed resource control strategies, with a focus on transactive energy and optimal dispatch.

Training

Courses for utility engineers, academics, and regulators covering GridLAB-D modeling, simulation, and LF Energy tools.
